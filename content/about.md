I am a freelance **Data Scientist with domain expertise in Security and Geopolitics**, based in Hamburg, Germany. 

I hold a PhD in Political Science with specialization in Strategic Studies from the University of Erfurt, Germany, and have acquired further specialized trainings in Data Science at the ReDI School of Digital Integration, Hamburg.

I have taught at several national and international academic institutions and have also collaborated extensively with research institutes and policy-oriented think tanks. These collaborations include work with the United Nations Development Programme in Pakistan, the Hanns Seidel Foundation in Pakistan, and the Heinrich Böll Foundation in Pakistan. Through these engagements, I have contributed to research and policy projects at the intersection of geopolitics, security studies, and data-driven analysis, particularly in relation to emerging technologies and contemporary conflict.

* **Methods:** Statistical Models, Machine Learning & AI, OSINT, Quantitative and Qualitative Research
