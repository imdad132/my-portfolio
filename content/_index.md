Today's geopolitical and security landscape is defined by complexity. Great power competition, hybrid warfare, drone operations, information campaigns, and rapid technological innovation are reshaping how power is exercised and contested. Linear, traditional analytical approaches are no longer sufficient to capture the essence and speed of modern strategic competition. To understand today’s shifts, therefore, one must analyze the multi-domain interactions and intersections of various actors and factors. 

At <strong>NexNode Security & Geopolitical Analytics</strong>, I integrate domain expertise in geopolitics and security studies with advanced data science methodologies to produce rigorous, evidence-based insights. Machine learning, computational text analysis, and quantitative modeling and forecasting enable deeper understanding of strategic competition, emerging threats, evolving patterns of conflict, and early warnings of brewing security challenges.
